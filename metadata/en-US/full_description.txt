BiliDl is an Android app for saving Bilibili videos that your account or the public page is allowed to access.

Features:
- Parse b23.tv, BV, av, ep, and ss links.
- Show quality options actually returned for each video.
- Download DASH video/audio and package the result as MP4.
- Save AAC audio-only streams as M4A.
- Parallel HTTP range downloads, verified checkpoints, and bounded retry.
- Bilibili QR authorization with credentials encrypted locally using Android Keystore.
- Background download tasks and progress notifications.
- Optional export of segmented Bilibili danmaku as XML.
- Optional download of BCC subtitles and conversion to SRT.

Current limits:
- Safe merging of multi-segment legacy DURL/FLV streams is not implemented.
- DASH sample extraction and muxing still use Android MediaExtractor/MediaMuxer.
- CI does not replace live CDN and on-device playback testing.

Only save content you are authorized to access and preserve, and respect creators' rights and Bilibili's rules.
